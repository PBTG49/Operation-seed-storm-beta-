Know what belongs.

The content layer is designed to be maintained separately from the UI. Replace the placeholder records with expert-reviewed species data.

01

Permission first

Use land you own or have permission to restore. Avoid protected or sensitive areas unless the responsible authority allows it.

02

Native first

Prefer locally appropriate native species and avoid introducing species simply because they grow quickly.

03

Care matters

Planting is only the beginning. Water, protection and follow-up can determine whether a restoration effort succeeds.
