Built for a
living planet.

Rootline is a community project concept for making ecological planting knowledge easier to discover, share and act on.

01

Ecology before borders.

The final database should be organized around ecological regions and local conditions rather than assuming a whole country shares one planting prescription.

02

Native before novelty.

Species recommendations should be reviewed for ecological fit, native status and local context before publication.

03

Permission before action.

Restoration should happen on land where the participant has appropriate permission, with care around protected and sensitive ecosystems.

04

Evidence before hype.

Reported numbers are not automatically “verified restoration.” The platform should label data honestly and show what was actually checked.

THE LONG-TERM IDEA

A simple website today can eventually become a global ecological reference layer: location → ecosystem → native species → planting guidance → community action.
